Built from the Stone It Stands On

Alder House opened in 1962 as a harbor-master's residence and has been welcoming guests, in one form or another, ever since. The rooms are small on purpose — every one faces water, and none of them feel like they belong to a chain.

Breakfast is served until eleven. The terrace bar opens at five, whenever the wind allows it.
